What changed in the approach

  • Developer equipment remains a reference. Public pre-raid sets and source-listed alternatives add actual gear trade-offs. Single-slot hit alternatives retain at least 80% of the source slot score and are labeled experimental combinations.
  • All races can select from the same faction-specific catalogue, including its Dwarf-aware sets. A hit racial can therefore change the preferred equipment.
  • The study compares developer timing, immediate racial use and, for Warriors, a 15-second delay. Night Elf and Dwarf Priests test their damage racial before the filler when native expected damage per execution time is higher. Skyborne mana users also test plain Read Leyline below 65% mana. Attuned and Charged are removed on raid entry, following the official clarification.
  • Unavailable-reference handling is a separate sensitivity treatment, with native metadata checks and saved before/after controls. It is not presented as proof of correct game behavior.
  • Both developer Mutilate builds enter after runtime cast and damage checks. The old warning text was insufficient grounds to exclude them.
  • Synthetic hit changes and Beast/Elemental checks are diagnostics. They do not enter the realistic pre-raid equipment pool.

Scope and limits

One stationary level-63 target, 3,731 armor, 60/180/300 seconds, shared consumables and developer talent builds. The full support case is an abstract buff package; the self case removes external buffs and debuffs. Developer swap sets and pet settings remain shared. Published Hunter sets use their supported source ammunition and quiver options; developer ranged inputs remain separately labeled controls. The melee Hunter uses a source-listed ammunition alternative and a 13% crafted-container scenario through the native effect controls. The source bag data omits haste; the Forever item tooltip independently confirms 13%. The native effect translation and ranged-input diagnostics are retained. This measures damage; it does not optimize a raid roster or value racial survival and utility.

Some public item IDs are absent from the native database. Their recorded replacements come from the source's ranked alternatives. No external item-stat line, EP estimate or synthetic Gunba item is injected into WoWSims. Novel procs, the provisional Revelation rate and other engine assumptions remain model uncertainties. Missing source coverage cannot be cured by more iterations.
