Methods, coverage and limits

  • Preset comparison: gear tiers, enchants, consumables, races, professions and latency differ. This measures the supplied configurations; it does not isolate class balance or establish best possible DPS.
  • Alpha models: all DPS entries are Alpha. Rotation and model limitations can exceed the displayed simulation uncertainty.
  • Common support: every run uses the same raid, party, individual buffs and target debuffs. External Innervate, Power Infusion, Mana Tide and Atiesh support are absent; self abilities remain available when a preset uses them.
  • Encounter: one level 63 mechanical target, 3,731 armor, fixed durations, no incoming attacks or assigned tank. No forced encounter movement; preset player movement, weaving and item swaps remain enabled. Execute phases: 20% and 35% of the fight.
  • Statistics: 10,000 iterations per cell; error bars are pointwise approximate 95% confidence intervals for the simulated mean, computed from saved samples. They describe Monte Carlo error, not model uncertainty. Small ordering differences should not be treated as proven rankings. All runs share seed 20261024; cross-class tests do not assume paired errors.
  • Coverage: 24 unique profiles across 11 DPS simulators and nine classes; 0 unsupported profiles are diagnostic only. Named build presets, uncovered talent presets and distinct defaults are included. Arbitrary talent combinations, every rotation variant and every gear combination are outside this baseline.
  • Duplicate requests removed: None. Original aliases are retained in profiles.json.
  • Damage accounting: player DPS already includes pets. Ability damage and cast counts are aggregated over all iterations; casts in the detail view are means per fight.
  • Debug screening: one 300-second debug iteration per profile; 3 profiles contain rejected cast attempts, usually resource or cooldown checks. These observations are preserved in the profile details and raw logs; this is not a full mechanics audit.
  • Gear policy: Equipment, enchants and item swaps follow developer presets. The pre-raid subset uses sets the developers label pre-raid or Pre-BiS; their acquisition rules and source caveats differ. Other profiles use P1, testing or placeholder sets, identified in the table and preset audit. Empty Warlock presets use the repository's pre-raid file recorded in the configuration.
  • Role coverage: This matrix covers DPS builds. Unsupported rotations are diagnostic. Healer and tank coverage is recorded in preset-audit.json; this report does not measure HPS or optimize a raid.
